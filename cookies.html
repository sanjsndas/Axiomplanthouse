🍪 Cookies & Digital Seeds Policy 🍪

🌱 Last revised: April 15, 2025 • Tiny, respectful crumbs for a better garden experience

At Axiom Plant House, we use small text files called cookies — think of them as harmless little seeds that help our digital greenhouse remember your preferences, ensure smooth navigation, and provide a serene browsing environment. This policy explains what cookies we plant, why we plant them, and how you can tend your own settings.

🌼 Our core belief: We never use cookies for intrusive cross-site tracking, advertising retargeting, or selling data. Cookies are used only to enhance your visit, like a gentle breeze in the garden.

1. What Are Cookies? (The Herbalist’s Definition)

Cookies are small data files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your device, remember actions or preferences, and improve functionality. Without cookies, many aspects of Axiom Plant House would feel less organic — like a garden without paths. We use both session cookies (which vanish when you close your browser) and persistent cookies (which remain for a defined period).

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

Our digital soil contains only essential, functional, and minimal analytical cookies — no invasive species.

3. Third-Party Cookies? Limited & With Transparency

Some pages may include embedded content from partner botanical gardens or educational seed libraries (e.g., a video about companion planting from a trusted non-profit). Those third-party services may place their own cookies, but we only collaborate with partners who respect data ethics. We encourage you to check their policies, though we limit these integrations to avoid intrusion. You remain in control.

🍃 Our promise: We do not allow any external advertisers or ad networks on Axiom Plant House. No banners, no tracking pixels. Your journey remains calm and pure.

4. Your Consent & Control — The Gardener’s Right

When you first visit our website, a small, unobtrusive cookie banner appears, asking for your permission to set non-essential cookies (analytics and functional preferences). Essential cookies are always active because the site needs them to work. You can change your mind anytime via our “Cookie Preferences” link available in the footer. Additionally, you can manage cookies through your browser settings:

  • ✔ Block all cookies (may affect site experience).
  • ✔ Delete existing cookies stored on your device.
  • ✔ Set alerts before cookies are placed.

To revise your consent with us directly, click the button below (simulated preference panel) — it will store your selection via a functional cookie. Alternatively, you can email privacy@axiomplanthouse.com with subject “Cookie Preferences”.

5. Managing Analytics — Privacy by Design

Our analytics use aggregated, anonymized data that never identifies individual users. We measure only page views, approximate country-level location (derived from IP but immediately anonymized), and referral sources. This helps us understand whether our "Moonlight Garden Tour" page inspires you. You can opt out of analytics cookies simply by rejecting non-essential cookies via the panel above. No personal data is ever linked to your session.

6. Cookie Lifespan & Retention

We keep cookies for the minimum time necessary: session cookies disappear after closing browser, persistent cookies (like preference settings) are kept up to 12 months, after which they may be renewed if you revisit. You can manually delete them anytime. We believe digital crumbs should decompose naturally, just like leaf litter.

7. Do Not Track (DNT) Signals

Axiom Plant House respects the global Do Not Track browser setting. If your browser sends a DNT signal, we will not deploy any non-essential analytics or functional cookies beyond strictly necessary ones. We automatically detect this preference and honor your choice.

8. Changes to This Cookies Policy

From time to time, we may update this policy to reflect changes in technology or regulations. The "Last revised" date at the top will indicate the latest version. Significant changes will be notified via a site banner. Your continued use of our site after such updates implies acceptance — but you can always withdraw consent for non-essential cookies.

Frequently Asked Questions: Digital Seeds

🍃 How do I block cookies in my browser?

You can adjust your browser settings (Chrome: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and other site data; Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security; Safari: Preferences → Privacy). Blocking all cookies might affect some features like remembering your basket if you purchased future seeds.

🍃 Will you ever sell my browsing data?

Absolutely never. We do not sell, rent, or exchange any data, including cookie-derived data. Your trust is our most treasured blossom.

🍃 Do you use flash cookies or local storage for tracking?

No. Only standard browser cookies and minimal local storage for preferences (like dark/light mode), but nothing used for fingerprinting.

🍃 What if I visit from the EU or California?

We fully comply with GDPR and CCPA. We offer granular consent, explicit opt-out, and respect your privacy rights. No "dark patterns" or pre-checked boxes — you are the master of your digital garden.
