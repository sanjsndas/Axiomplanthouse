◈ Arrival & Garden Etiquette ◈

Our nursery is nestled within the Axiom Black & Cream Arboretum — a living museum of rare dark foliage and ivory blossoms. When you visit, you'll find self-guided trails, ‘silence zones’ for pollinator observation, and the Cream Pot Café serving herbal infusions. We encourage visitors to bring a journal and sketch the interplay of shadow and light. Wheelchair accessible paths, and children accompanied by adults are welcome to participate in seed-planting activities. For large groups (10+), kindly notify us at least 3 days ahead using our contact form or hotline. Also, every first Saturday of the month we host a “Compost & Cultivation” open forum — free and open to all who cherish the living soil.

📣 Nursery news: Starting June 2025, Axiom Plant House introduces a “Black Gold Soil Clinic” — bring a sample from your garden, and our soil sommeliers will prescribe custom amendments, from cream-hued calcined clay to mushroom compost. Walk-in Wednesdays 1–4 PM.
