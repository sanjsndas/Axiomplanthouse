◈ Privacy & Earthkeeper's Pledge ◈

🌿 Last revised: April 15, 2025 • Rooted in transparency

At Axiom Plant House, we believe that your personal data is like a delicate cream blossom — deserving of reverence, protection, and the richest soil of integrity. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our digital greenhouse, engage with our nursery, or correspond with our cultivators.

🌼 Our guiding principle: We never sell, trade, or rent your personal data. Our relationship is rooted in mutual growth — your trust is the water that nourishes our practice.

1. Information We Gather

When you interact with Axiom Plant House — whether sending a message, joining a workshop, subscribing to the Cream Envelope, or purchasing heirloom seeds — we may collect:

  • 🌱 Contact details: name, email address, phone number, postal address (for deliveries).
  • 📜 Communication history: messages, gardening questions, consultation preferences.
  • 🌍 Technical data: IP address, browser type, and aggregate site usage (via cookies – see our Cookies Policy).
  • 🍃 Transactional info: if you purchase plants, seeds, or merchandise, we process payment data through secure partners (we never store full payment credentials).

2. How We Use Your Information

Like a gardener tending to each seedling, we use your data to:

  • ✔ Respond to inquiries, provide horticultural advice and soil clinic recommendations.
  • ✔ Process nursery visits, workshop registrations, and “Moonlight Garden” tour bookings.
  • ✔ Send seasonal Cream Envelopes (only with your explicit subscription) — seed packets, planting guides, and botanical notes.
  • ✔ Improve our website, develop new offerings (e.g. Black Gold Soil Clinic), and ensure security.
  • ✔ Communicate about order updates, essential policy changes, or urgent nursery alerts.

We only process data when we have a lawful basis: your consent, contractual necessity, or legitimate interests (like improving our sustainable services).

3. Sharing & Disclosure — Our Circle of Trust

We do not sell or rent your information. Limited sharing occurs only in these specific, protected circumstances:

  • 🔹 Service partners: trusted shipping carriers (to send your heirloom seeds), secure payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), and email dispatch platforms (for newsletters). These partners comply with strict data protection.
  • 🔹 Legal obligations: if required by law, court order, or to defend our rights — but we will always strive to notify you beforehand when permitted.
  • 🔹 With your explicit consent: for collaborative workshops or events with other botanical institutions (you will be asked separately).

Our regional envoys (fernguild@axiom.com, etc.) operate under the same confidentiality oath.

4. Data Retention & Black Soil Stewardship

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described, or as required by tax/commerce laws (e.g., transaction records for 7 years). Typically:

  • 📬 Inquiry messages: up to 3 years after last contact, to preserve continuity of garden advice.
  • 📦 Purchase history: retained for warranty, returns, and seed germination references.
  • 📧 Newsletter subscription: until you withdraw consent (unsubscribe link in every email).

After retention period, data is securely anonymized or deleted — returning to the digital earth, just as leaves compost into new growth.

5. Your Rights — as a Guardian of Your Own Garden

Depending on your location (including GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California), you have the right to:

  • 🌿 Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • 🌿 Rectify inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • 🌿 Erase your data (“right to be forgotten”), subject to legal obligations.
  • 🌿 Restrict or object to processing (e.g., marketing).
  • 🌿 Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a structured format.
  • 🌿 Withdraw consent at any time for email marketing or analytics cookies.

To exercise any right, please contact our Data Steward at privacy@axiomplanthouse.com or via mail to the address in footer. We will respond within 30 days (often sooner). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

🌸 Children’s privacy: Our nursery and website are intended for general audiences, but we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent. If we learn of such collection, we will delete it promptly.

6. Security — Protecting the Cream Blossom

We implement technical and organizational measures: encryption (SSL/TLS), access controls, regular security reviews, and staff training in data privacy. While no method is 100% invulnerable, we continuously adapt to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. Our digital soil is fortified with modern safeguards.

7. Third-Party Links & Herbal Collaborations

Our website may include links to partner botanical gardens, organic seed banks, or educational resources. Please note that once you leave Axiom Plant House, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to read the privacy statements of any external site, as we cannot accept responsibility for their practices.

8. International Transfers

Axiom Plant House is rooted in multiple regions (US, UK, EU). Your data may be transferred and stored in countries with different data protection laws. When we transfer data internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or adequacy decisions. Wherever your data travels, it remains under the same level of care described here.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

As our garden evolves, we may update this policy. The “Last revised” date at the top will indicate modifications. If changes are significant (e.g., new data uses), we’ll provide prominent notice via email (if we have your address) or a banner on our website. Your continued interaction with Axiom Plant House constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

Additional Protections for Sensitive Data

Complaint resolution: If you believe we have mishandled your data, please contact us first — we will work to resolve the issue amicably. You may also contact the ICO (UK) or your local data protection authority.
