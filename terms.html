◈ 1. Information We Collect: The Minimal Harvest

In the spirit of low-intervention gardening, we collect only the essential data required to tend our community. (c) Cookies and similar technologies: we utilize only functional and analytical cookies (no advertising trackers). See our Cookies Policy for full details. (d) Community forums: any content you voluntarily post (garden photos, discussions) becomes part of the living archive; you retain copyright but grant Axiom a perpetual license to display and preserve it within our ecosystem. You may request deletion at any time via our Root Support channel.

📋 Minimal Harvest Pledge — We never collect: precise geolocation, biometric data, genetic information, political or religious affiliations, or browsing history outside axiomplanthouse.com. Your data belongs to you, just as each seed belongs to the earth that nurtures it.
